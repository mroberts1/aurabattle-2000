How to play

Each round you and your opponent pick a move in secret. Then the ring votes. Losing the vote costs aura; hit 0 and you're cooked.

Pose: solid hype. Cracks if Clowned.
Hold: stone face, Clown bounces off. Holding twice with nothing coming = NPC, -1.
Clown: crack them up (-1 and the crowd turns).
Stare Down: breaks a Hold, loses to a Pose.
Farm: slow walk, shades on, +2 aura. Cracks if Clowned.
Clip Farm: play to the phones. Big if the ring buys it, -3 if they don't.

Don't pick in time and you hesitate (-1). Most judges reward composure over effort. Keys 1-6 pick moves.
